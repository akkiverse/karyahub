# KaryaHub: Risks and challenges

**Version:** 1.0, CR-0002 (2026-10-28). The founder asked for the risks and challenges of taking KaryaHub further, each with how to resolve it. Likelihood and impact are High, Medium or Low. "Status" says what CR-0002 already did.

| ID | Risk or challenge | Likelihood | Impact | How we resolve it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | **Meta business verification and WhatsApp onboarding take weeks** and need a registered company; without it there is no real intake or sending (the reference platform is blocked on exactly this). | High | High | Start verification early; use Meta's test number for development; keep the web app fully usable without WhatsApp; partner BSP as a fallback. | Mitigated in design: the webhook, signature check and adapters are built and tested with mock data, so going live is configuration plus the live adapter. |
| R-02 | **WhatsApp costs and policy change** (per-template pricing, possible charges for service replies, the January 2026 ban on general-purpose AI chat bots). | Medium | High | KaryaHub is a task bot, not a chat bot: one structured reply per report. Use utility templates only, meter every message per firm, batch customer updates, include a message allowance in pricing. | Open: metering comes with the database. |
| R-03 | **Wrong extraction** from Hinglish voice notes or messy job-sheet photos (names, phone numbers, dates, amounts). | High | Medium | Owner always approves a draft (already the flow); show the transcript next to the fields; validate phone, date and amount; keep sample-based regression tests; pick a speech model tuned for Indian languages (for example Sarvam AI). | Partly done: approval and validation exist; server extraction is tested against the demo samples. CR-0004: real AI extraction with a JSON schema at temperature 0, every AI answer validated (phones, dates, amounts), and a reader tag on each draft so the owner knows how it was read. |
| R-04 | **Duplicate jobs** when Meta retries a webhook or a request is sent twice. | High | Medium | Idempotency at every step: message id de-duplication, client operation ids, deterministic event ids, unique visit keys. Across deliveries this needs a stored set of seen ids. | Done within one delivery and for workflow and visits; cross-delivery de-duplication arrives with the database (unique index on message id). |
| R-05 | **Slow webhook processing** (speech plus LLM can take seconds) makes Meta retry and floods duplicates. | Medium | Medium | Answer 200 fast: store the raw event, process in a background job, reply to the technician when done. On Vercel, use a queue (for example Vercel Queues or a Postgres job table) once there is a database. | Open: demo processing is instant; documented in HLD roadmap. |
| R-06 | **Webhook spoofing**: anyone can post fake messages to a public URL. | Medium | High | Verify `X-Hub-Signature-256` over the raw body with the app secret before parsing; constant-time compares; refuse in live mode without a secret. | Done (AC-8.2). |
| R-07 | **Guessing customer codes**: a 6-digit code has a million options; without attempt limits it could be brute-forced. | Low | Medium | 10-minute expiry and binding to job and tenant (done); add attempt counting, single use and per-job lockout in the database; owner-verified fallback with a reason (`supervisor_verify`) instead of retrying. | Partly done. |
| R-08 | **Sign-in strength** (changed CR-0005): accounts are now real and on the server, with salted PIN hashes, lock-out after 5 tries and temporary PINs. What remains: a 4 to 6 digit PIN is guessable by someone who can try from many numbers, there is no second factor, and forgot-PIN codes can't reach real numbers until WhatsApp is live. | Medium | High | Lock-out per number, hash cost, no weak PINs, owners reset PINs; add a second factor or longer secrets before sensitive data; connect WhatsApp for codes. | Partly done (CR-0005). |
| R-09 | **Fake proof of work** (technician reports work that was not done). | Medium | High | Customer one-time code as the main proof (done); later camera-only photos with hashes, location at report time and a review queue for doubtful jobs, as in the reference risk score. | Partly done. |
| R-10 | **Data leaks between businesses** (changed CR-0005): people and teams now share one database; a missing `business_id` filter would leak them. | Medium | High | Every query is scoped by the signed-in person's business; isolation tests for team, reset, permissions and the audit log; Postgres row-level security when jobs and customers move to the server (CR-0007). | Partly done (CR-0005). |
| R-11 | **Personal data and the DPDP Act** (customer names and phone numbers; obligations in full from May 2027). | Medium | High | Collect only what jobs need; hash phones for matching; privacy notice and consent text in WhatsApp; retention limits; export and delete on request; Indian data region when the database is added. | Partly done (hashing, no logging of phones or codes). |
| R-12 | **GST and invoice correctness** (numbering per financial year, CGST/SGST versus IGST, HSN/SAC codes, e-invoicing thresholds). | Medium | Medium | Server computes totals in paise; IGST for inter-state is supported; documents stay watermarked "DEMO" until a chartered accountant reviews the format; e-invoicing through a GST provider later. | Partly done. |
| R-13 | **Stateless hosting** (Vercel Functions keep nothing between requests) means data lives in one browser. | High | Medium | Keep the engine pure so storage can be swapped; add Postgres from the Vercel Marketplace with Alembic migrations; browser data stays readable through versioned migrations. | Prepared (pure engine, data migration v1 to v2). |
| R-14 | **Two places with the same rules** (browser fallback and server) can drift. | Medium | Low | The server is the source of truth; the browser keeps only the demo fallback for Chat; `data/samples.json` is generated from the same demo data; tests check both give the AC-3.2 fields. Remove the fallback once the server is always reachable. | Mitigated. |
| R-15 | **Technician adoption**: field staff resist new tools and feeling watched. | Medium | High | No app to install: WhatsApp only, voice first, Hindi and Hinglish, one reply per report; show technicians their own jobs; start with the lead technician. | Product approach; Hindi UI is on the roadmap. |
| R-16 | **Scope creep** toward a full field-service suite (inventory, attendance, billing) like the reference platform. | Medium | Medium | Hold KaryaHub to its promise (reports to jobs, paperwork, proof, renewals); every new area needs a change request and a business reason. | Ongoing. |
| R-17 | **Vendor lock-in for speech and LLM** and their cost per message. | Low | Medium | Adapters with one interface each; log usage per firm; prefer models priced for Indian languages; cache sample results in demos. | Done (adapters). CR-0004: the LLM runs on the founder's own server through Studio AI (no per-message cost, no app keys); switching to a paid OpenAI-compatible service is a setting (`AI_API=openai`). |
| R-18 | **The AI server is one PC** (added CR-0004): when it's off, asleep or out of memory, AI reading stops; a 9B model can also misread messy Hinglish or handwriting. | High | Medium | Fail over through `AI_ENDPOINTS` (a second PC can be added), fall back to the quick reader with a clear "Read without AI" label and banner, keep owner approval on every draft, and limit each AI call to 40 seconds. Move to an always-on host or a paid service once real firms depend on it. | Done for the demo: fallback, labels, banner and tests; always-on hosting is a later decision. |
| R-19 | **Lost secrets**: if `KARYAHUB_PIN_PEPPER` is lost or changed every PIN stops working; if `KARYAHUB_BOOTSTRAP_TOKEN` leaks someone could reset the platform admin's PIN. | Low | High | Keep both in the password manager and Vercel only; the token is only needed for setup and recovery and can be removed afterwards; rotate by resetting PINs. | Open. |
| R-20 | **One database for every firm** (added CR-0005): an outage or a bad migration affects all businesses. | Low | High | Neon backups and point-in-time restore; migrations run once under a lock and are additive; a health check reports `database`. | Open. |
| R-21 | **Jobs stay on the device** (added CR-0005): a real firm's jobs, customers and documents are per device until CR-0007, so clearing the browser or switching phones loses them. | High | Medium | Say so plainly; CR-0007 moves them to the server; owners can keep copies of documents. | Open until CR-0007. |

## Top three to act on next
1. **R-21 jobs on the server** (CR-0007) and **R-10 row-level security** with it, before real firms rely on KaryaHub for their job data.
2. **R-01 Meta verification**: start now, it has the longest lead time.
3. **R-05 and R-04**: background processing and stored de-duplication, which come together with the database.
| R-22 | **Two people change the same record while offline** (added CR-0009): the later sync wins for ordinary fields, so one change can be lost. | Medium | Low | Job state, history and customer codes are the server's and cannot be overwritten; chat messages and documents are written by one person at a time in practice; the app shows rejections and the server's version; field-level merge only if real firms hit this. | Accepted (ADR-0013). |
| R-23 | **Row-level security may not be available** (added CR-0009): the host may refuse to create the restricted database role, or the connection role may bypass policies. | Low | High | Every statement is already filtered by business; the migration tries the role and policies inside savepoints and never blocks start-up; `/api/health` reports `row_level_security`; a test runs the wall on a real Postgres when an address is given. | Open until `/api/health` says `on` on the live database. |
| R-24 | **Customer data on phones and in the database** (added CR-0009): customer names and numbers are personal data (DPDP) and now sit in one shared database and in each phone's copy. | Medium | High | Business wall and permissions; no data in logs; sign-out clears nothing but the next person on a shared phone only gets their own business's copy; export and delete on request and a privacy notice come with K8. | Open. |
